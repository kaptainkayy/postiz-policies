Privacy Policy

How KaptainKay handles information when using its self-hosted Postiz installation to manage and schedule social media content.

About this service

KaptainKay uses a self-hosted Postiz installation to manage and schedule content for social media accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage. It is a personal-use service and is not open to public sign-up. This page describes what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information the service handles

Which information is processed depends on the permissions granted when an account is connected. Depending on those permissions, the service may handle:

  • Account details — account IDs, usernames, and profile information.
  • Authorization tokens — the access granted when an account is connected.
  • Content — the posts, media, and schedules supplied for publishing.
  • Post information — details about published posts, such as engagement statistics and other analytics the connected platform makes available.
  • Technical logs — records kept for security and troubleshooting.

The service does not ask for the password of a social media account. Connections are made through each platform’s own authorization process.

How the information is used

The information is used to:

  • connect the accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage;
  • prepare and publish the content supplied for those accounts;
  • display the analytics each platform makes available; and
  • maintain and operate the service.

Storage and sharing

Information handled by the service is stored on infrastructure controlled by the operator. Authorized content and the account information needed for a requested feature are exchanged with the connected platforms — for example, to publish a post or to retrieve its statistics.

Retention and deletion

The service is for personal use only, and information is retained while it is needed to operate the connected accounts.

Deletion requests sent to thekaptainkay@gmail.com are reviewed within 30 days. This is a commitment to review each request, not a promise that deletion will be finished within that period.

Backup copies may remain in place until they are replaced through normal backup rotation.

Revoking access

Access granted to the service can be revoked through the settings of each connected platform. Disconnecting an account does not automatically delete content that has already been published on the platform.

Security

The service is protected with reasonable access controls and other safeguards. As with any online service, no method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure, and no absolute guarantee can be made.

Contact and changes

Questions and requests can be sent to thekaptainkay@gmail.com. When this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new effective date.

Personal-use service

Social media management, run on self-hosted Postiz

KaptainKay uses a self-hosted Postiz installation to prepare, schedule, and manage posts for social media accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage. This page explains what the service is and how it works.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service

What the service does

Posts move through a simple workflow inside the operator’s Postiz dashboard:

Step 1

Connect authorized accounts

An account is linked through the platform’s own authorization process, using the permissions that platform grants. Only accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage are connected, and access can be revoked in the platform’s settings at any time.

Step 2

Prepare content

Posts, captions, and media for the connected accounts are prepared and organized in the dashboard ahead of time.

Step 3

Schedule and review

Content is scheduled for a chosen time, and the published result is reviewed on the destination platform afterwards. Scheduling does not guarantee publication — outages, platform restrictions, and API limits can delay or prevent a post, as the Terms of Service describe.

Which platforms the service can work with depends on the permissions each platform grants — the Terms of Service list examples such as TikTok, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google (YouTube), and X.

A private, operator-controlled service

KaptainKay is a personal-use service. There is no public sign-up, no public app, and no customer accounts. The Postiz dashboard behind the service is a private deployment, controlled by the operator and reachable only by the authorized operator over a private network.

This page is public information about the service. It does not open the dashboard to visitors: reading it grants no access, approval, or right to use the service.

Intended use with TikTok

The service is intended to prepare and schedule video content for a TikTok account that the operator owns or is authorized to manage, using TikTok’s platform interfaces. Any connection to TikTok, and any posting through it, requires TikTok’s approval and the permissions TikTok grants.

KaptainKay is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by TikTok, and nothing on this page should be read as a statement that a TikTok account is currently connected or that TikTok has approved the service.
